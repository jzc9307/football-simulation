# Contract snapshot

Starting expiry years and weekly GBP wages come from the public [FUTWIZ FC26 career database](https://www.futwiz.com/fc26/career-mode/players), retrieved on 28 September 2026. These are game-database values, not a claim about current legal contracts. Expiry years are represented as 30 June of that year because the source provides a year, not a day. Already-ended source terms are visibly labelled; they are not silently extended.

5,182 of the 6,473 unique players in this project's rosters have a confident source match. Missing or ambiguous records now receive **Simulated terms**, explicitly separate from sourced terms: older players receive one year, other players two or three, and wages reflect rating, age, potential and the club's finances. Existing verified wages and expiry years are preserved. Names and starting clubs are not changed. Haaland's verified alternative spelling has an explicit source match. Height, foot, nationality and five ability fields also come from matched records; goalkeeper fields use goalkeeper labels.

`scripts/import-contracts.mjs` queries the public FC26 search table and prints the proposed JSON snapshot to stdout for review. It does not write files. The site currently hosts that table through its FC27 route; the action requests the FC26 database explicitly. Full/common-name matching is followed by conservative age/position/team checks, and ambiguous ties are left unmatched. The public action identifier may change; stop and inspect the source if it becomes unavailable.

New contracts negotiated during play have `source: career`. Their wages, promises and terms are simulation values. Career statistics come exclusively from this game's simulated matches.

Existing player wages are funded by a fixed board allocation at season start. A new signing, or the wage increase on a renewal, transfers its remaining-season payroll cost from available transfer funds into the wage reserve. Weekly payroll spends that reserve every seven career days; it does not charge the transfer funds a second time. Rejected terms cost nothing, and unused transfer-funded wages are returned when a player leaves. The Budget Overview distinguishes allocations from actual spending. Loan wages remain with the owning club. New seasons receive a fresh board allocation for the squad already under contract.

Playing-time warnings follow contracted roles. Key players warn after two consecutive matches without meaningful minutes, starters after three, rotation players after six and prospects after ten. Four more ignored matches force a transfer request and happiness falls to zero. Injured/suspended absence does not count. Three meaningful appearances can restore trust. Formal requests allow off-window approaches, but an agreed transfer registers only when the next transfer window opens. Happy players in the final two contract years receive a renewal invitation. Expired contracts create free agents; suitable AI clubs can recruit them without a fee.
